TATA DATA VISUALISATION INTERNSHIP - TASK 1: FRAMING THE BUSINESS SCENARIO
Questions to prepare for the CEO and CMO meeting

Dataset reviewed: Online Retail (541,909 transaction lines, 25,900 invoices, 4,372 identified customers, 4,070 products, 38 countries, 1 Dec 2010 - 9 Dec 2011). Fields: InvoiceNo, StockCode, Description, Quantity, InvoiceDate, UnitPrice, CustomerID, Country.

--------------------------------------------------------------
QUESTIONS FOR THE CEO (operations, growth, financial health)
--------------------------------------------------------------

1. How has revenue and order volume trended month by month, and which months and days/hours drive the peaks?
   Why it matters: Shows the seasonality and growth trajectory behind next year's targets, and shows when to plan stock, staffing and fulfilment capacity. (Revenue = Quantity x UnitPrice, tracked alongside number of invoices and average order value.)

2. Which products and product categories generate the most revenue, and how concentrated is revenue? Do a small number of items carry the business?
   Why it matters: Tells leadership what is working and should be protected or scaled, and which long-tail items tie up inventory without paying back. Quantity sold should be compared with revenue to separate high-volume low-price items from high-value ones.

3. Which countries or regions contribute the most revenue, and which are growing fastest outside the home market?
   Why it matters: The business appears heavily dependent on the United Kingdom. This answers the expansion question: where to expand, and how risky the dependence on one market is. Compare total revenue, average order value and growth rate per country.

4. How much revenue is lost to returns and cancellations (negative quantities / credit notes), and which products or countries are most affected?
   Why it matters: Returns are a hidden operational cost that erodes net revenue and signals product quality, description or fulfilment problems. Net revenue, not gross, should be the basis for planning and expansion decisions.

--------------------------------------------------------------
QUESTIONS FOR THE CMO (customers, retention, marketing effectiveness)
--------------------------------------------------------------

1. Who are our most valuable customers, and how can we segment them (for example using RFM: Recency, Frequency, Monetary value)?
   Why it matters: Lets marketing target high-value customers, win back lapsing ones and tailor campaigns by segment. It also shows what share of revenue comes from the top 10-20% of customers.

2. What is our customer retention picture: how many customers are new versus repeat, and how does repeat purchasing change over time (cohort analysis)?
   Why it matters: Retaining a customer is cheaper than acquiring one. Cohort retention and repeat purchase rate show whether marketing is building loyalty or only buying one-off sales, and what customer lifetime value looks like.

3. Which customer groups (by country, as the available demographic proxy) buy the most, spend the most per order and buy the broadest range of products?
   Why it matters: Supports geographic targeting and localised campaigns. Average order value, items per basket and purchase frequency by country show where marketing spend is likely to earn the best return.

4. Which products are bought together and what time patterns (month, weekday, hour) are best for promotions?
   Why it matters: Market basket analysis supports bundles, cross-sell and upsell. Timing analysis shows when to send campaigns so they meet peak buying behaviour, such as the pre-Christmas surge, and how to lift average order value.

--------------------------------------------------------------
DATA NOTES TO RAISE WITH LEADERSHIP
--------------------------------------------------------------
- About 25% of rows (135,080) have no CustomerID, so customer-level analysis (retention, RFM) covers only identified customers. Suggest improving customer ID capture at checkout.
- Demographic data is limited to Country; age, gender or channel data are not available. Recommend collecting them, plus marketing channel/source, to measure campaign ROI.
- About 10,600 lines have negative quantities (returns/cancellations) and some have zero or negative prices; these need cleaning or separate treatment.
- The data covers only about 13 months, so year-on-year growth cannot be measured. December 2011 is a partial month (to 9 Dec).
